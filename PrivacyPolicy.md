# **Study Ten  - Privacy Policy**

**Last Updated: May 1, 2025**

---

## **1. Overview**
**Study Ten Hours** (referred to as "the App") respects and protects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information, including details about iCloud synchronization. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

---

## **2. Information We Collect**
### **2.1 Collected Information**
The App collects the following data for iCloud synchronization:
- Countdown timer settings
- App preferences (e.g., theme color, sound toggle)

### **2.2 Information We Don't Collect**
The App **does not** collect:
- Personally identifiable information (PII)
- Device identifiers
- Location data
- Any other personally identifiable data

---

## **3. iCloud Data Synchronization**
### **3.1 Sync Functionality**
- Uses iCloud Drive to sync your countdown settings and preferences
- Requires explicit user authorization
- Data is transmitted and stored using Apple's encrypted services

### **3.2 Data Storage**
- Synced data is stored in your private iCloud container
- Data is only visible to your own devices
- We cannot access this data

### **3.3 Disabling Sync**
You can disable synchronization:
1. Device Settings > [Your Apple ID] > iCloud
2. Toggle off "Study Ten" sync

---

## **4. Data Security**
- All synced data uses Apple's end-to-end encryption
- Local data uses iOS secure storage mechanisms
- We do not store or transmit your data through any other means

---

## **5. Data Deletion**
- Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete iCloud data
- To completely remove data:
  1. Clear all data within the App
  2. Delete related data in iCloud settings

---

## **6. Children's Privacy**
The App is **not directed at children under 13** and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

---

## **7. Policy Changes**
We may update this policy periodically. The "Last Updated" date will reflect changes.

---

## **8. Your Rights**
- **Access Data**: View all locally stored data in the App
- **Delete Data**: Uninstall to remove local data
- **Withdraw Consent**: Not applicable as we don't collect personal data
